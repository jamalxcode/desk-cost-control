# Regional Addendum: Red Sea & Bab el-Mandeb Watch Desk Stub (`red-sea.md`)

## Scope & Priority
- Primary Focus: Southern Red Sea, Bab el-Mandeb Strait, Gulf of Aden, Yemeni western coastal waters, Hodeidah port, missile/drone launch points in western Yemen.
- Immediate Alert Triggers (`ALERT` / `🔴`):
  - Anti-ship ballistic missile (ASBM) or cruise missile launch toward maritime traffic.
  - One-way attack drone (OWA-UAV) or uncrewed surface vessel (USV) strike on commercial/coalition vessels.
  - Coalition interception of anti-ship weapons or defensive strikes on launch sites.

## Quiet Policy
- When UKMTO/Ambrey report zero active incidents and commercial shipping routes proceed without disruption, emit silence (`items: []`).
