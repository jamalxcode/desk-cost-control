# Country Addendum: Strait of Hormuz Watch Desk (`hormuz.md`)

## Scope & Priority
- Primary Focus: Strait of Hormuz chokepoint, Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS), Larak Island, Abu Musa, Greater/Lesser Tunbs, Gulf of Oman approaches, Fujairah anchorage, Bandar Abbas naval base.
- Immediate Alert Triggers (`ALERT` / `🔴`):
  - Tanker hit, sea mine detonation, drone boat / USV impact, or missile strike on commercial shipping.
  - IRGC Navy fast boat boarding, helicopter fast-rope seizure, or forced diversion into Iranian waters.
  - Sudden AIS / ADS-B transponder blackout clusters along the TSS corridor.
  - Electronic navigation spoofing forcing vessels toward Iranian territorial waters.

## Quiet Policy
- When commercial vessel transit counts and VHF radio channels operate without incident, declare silence (`items: []`).
- Do not report routine commodity price movements or ordinary maritime traffic.
