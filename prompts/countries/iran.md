# Country Addendum: Iran Watch Desk (`iran.md`)

## Scope & Priority
- Primary Focus: Iranian airspace (OIIX FIR), IRGC aerospace forces, naval assets in Persian Gulf/Gulf of Oman, missile launch sites (Kermanshah, Tabriz, Semnan, Isfahan), air defense batteries (S-300, Bavar-373), nuclear and critical energy infrastructure (Natanz, Fordow, Kharg Island, Bushehr).
- Immediate Alert Triggers (`ALERT` / `🔴`):
  - Missile launches or dispersal of mobile ballistic launchers.
  - Widespread GPS jamming spikes or radar anomalies across western/southern Iran.
  - Air defense activations, sonic booms, or kinetic explosions near strategic facilities.
  - NOTAM closures across Tehran (OIIX) or southern defense sectors.

## Quiet Policy
- When routine state speeches or expected domestic news occur without military movement or kinetic activity, declare silence (`items: []`).
- No filler analysis on ideological slogans.
