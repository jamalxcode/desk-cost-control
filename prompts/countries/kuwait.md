# Country Addendum: Kuwait Watch Desk (`kuwait.md`)

## Scope & Priority
- Primary Focus: Kuwait territory, Kuwait FIR (OKAC), border areas with Iraq and Saudi Arabia, Failaka Island, Bubiyan Island, offshore oil platforms, Ali Al Salem Air Base, Camp Arifjan.
- Immediate Alert Triggers (`ALERT` / `🔴`):
  - Kuwait Air Defense / Patriot battery activations or missile intercepts.
  - Civil defense sirens sounding in Kuwait City, Jahra, Ahmadi, or any governorate.
  - OKAC FIR airspace restrictions, civil aviation holding patterns, or diversions from Kuwait International (KWI).
  - Cross-border drone intrusions or maritime incursions into Kuwaiti territorial waters.

## Quiet Policy
- When no sirens, intercepts, military scrambles, or airspace disruptions occur, declare silence (`items: []`).
- Do not summarize routine municipal or domestic political statements.
